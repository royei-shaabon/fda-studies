This is the closest rehearsal yet to your actual take-home. You're going to build a small version of an "airport investment scoring" tool, end to end.

Your task — build a full pipeline:

Write three separate, named scoring criterion functions (each isolated, each explainable on its own):
Passenger volume (bigger = more expansion justification)
Delay rate (higher = more congestion pressure, i.e. treat this as a positive signal for "needs expansion" this time — not a penalty like before; think about why the sign flips depending on what question you're answering)
Long-haul flight percentage (higher = more international/long-haul demand, a positive signal)
Write a combined scoreCandidate function that takes a weights object with all three criteria and returns a total score.
Produce a ranked list (highest score first) of all four airports.
For the top-ranked airport, write a short human-readable explanation string that names the specific factors driving its score (e.g. "Boston Logan ranks highest due to high passenger volume (12.5M) and strong long-haul demand (22%), despite elevated delays (18%).") — this should be generated from the actual data, not hardcoded text.
Run it and show me the full output: the ranked list and the explanation string.
