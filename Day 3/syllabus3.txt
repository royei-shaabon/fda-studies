Structured Data → Using It

This is the second half of the pattern your technical interview names directly — you've spent two days getting messy data into clean, typed structures. Now: writing logic that actually does something with it — ranking, scoring, aggregating, answering a specific question. This is also a direct rehearsal of your take-home's "deterministic scoring or ranking logic" requirement.

Day 3 syllabus:

1	Core array methods for real work: map, filter, reduce, sort
2	Aggregation / grouping (Excel pivot-table / SQL GROUP BY equivalent)
3	Writing a scoring function with explicit, explainable criteria
4	Ranking/sorting by score, returning a clean, explainable result (not just raw numbers)
5	Capstone: score and rank a small structured dataset against multiple criteria, and produce a human-readable explanation of the ranking — direct rehearsal of your take-home
